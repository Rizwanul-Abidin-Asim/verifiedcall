AI · the conversation

The voice leg is its own model (Gemini 2.5 Flash via Vapi): it picks the next scripted line, never composes one, and hangs up with a tool call. Answers are extracted to yes / no / unclear; hesitation is timed from the transcript.

human in the loop

Analyst override on the fraud desk: release or block, with a reason. Recorded beside the agent's verdict, never over it — so the bank can ask how often it overrules the agent, and whether it was right.

audit

Every CAMARA call, every decision, every call transcript and every override is persisted. A cached response is always labelled cached. A demo persona that draws on several sandbox numbers says so on screen.
